01 — Direction

Consult, build, deploy.

Mahber Studio is a team of developers and other professionals. They consult businesses, startups, and individuals on what software they need, then they build it and deploy it. The product belongs to the client. It is not a subscription product.

This file is a preview of the system already on the site: ink ground, cream panels, lime action, hairline rules, Space Grotesk, and IBM Plex Mono. The live pages are unchanged.

02 — Color

One palette, two surfaces.

The page is ink. Cream is for a product shown inside the page, such as a client website. Lime is the only action color. Clay marks a pause, not a second brand.

03 — Type

Grotesk for the sentence. Mono for the label.

Headlines are Space Grotesk 500, tracking -0.03em, line-height 0.94. Labels, indexes, and eyebrows are IBM Plex Mono, 11–12px, uppercase, tracking 0.08em. ማህበር stays in Noto Sans Ethiopic and is not set in the mono style.

04 — Space and radius

A short scale. Almost no rounding.

Spacing steps are 4, 8, 12, 16, 22, 28, 40, 64, and 96. Page padding is clamp(20px, 3vw, 40px). The nav is 64px. Buttons are 2px. Inputs and frames are square. 28px is reserved for a phone bezel, not for cards.
